THE SOFTEST KIND OF TRUST

He knows her—even
after the betrayal.

By the fire, they learn to move through the threads together. He worries when she gets hurt. She misses the signs that he’s falling for her. And when he discovers she was sent to deceive him, he understands the person beneath the mission.

He is wounded, but his heart stays open. In a story full of castles, conquest, and danger, that quiet understanding feels like its own kind of magic.
